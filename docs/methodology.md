# Benchmarking methodology

## Unit of analysis

The core mart contains one row per six-character CMS Certification Number (CCN). Quality and
experience observations remain measure-level in fact tables before explicitly selected measures
are rolled up. The latest record within the 2023 cost-report product is selected by fiscal-year
end and report record number.

## Comparability

The project does not combine unlike measures into a universal score. Quality, experience,
episode-spending, utilization, and accounting KPIs are displayed separately with their source
periods. A joined row does not imply identical measurement windows or populations.

## Outlier detection

Expense-per-discharge and overall-rating percentiles are calculated within hospital-type and
bed-size peers. A minimum of 10 non-null peers is required. The top cost decile and bottom
rating decile are descriptive review flags. They are sensitive to data coverage, reporting,
case mix, and accounting differences and should prompt investigation rather than conclusions.

## Validation

Critical checks enforce unique and valid CCNs, one cost record per hospital, nonnegative beds
and discharges, and the 1–5 rating domain. Financial reconciliation compares total assets with
total liabilities and fund balances using the larger of $1,000 or 1% of assets as tolerance.
Coverage gaps are reported as informational because CMS suppression and eligibility can apply.
